Profiles

Each profile keeps its own bookmarks, history, passwords, cookies, settings and sync login, and opens in its own window. A profile can have a password that is asked before it opens.

A profile password keeps other people from opening that profile in mbrowser. It does not encrypt the profile’s files on disk; saved passwords have their own, separate master password and are encrypted. Opening a profile starts a new mbrowser window group; close the one you no longer need.
