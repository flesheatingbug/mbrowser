Features

Everything you expect. Nothing you don't.

A browser built around privacy, speed and getting out of your way.

🧩

Speed dial start page

Visual tiles with folders, several pages, wallpapers (even online ones) and drag-and-drop, the same on every device.

🛡️

Built-in privacy

Ad and tracker blocking, cookie pop-up hiding, private windows and per-site controls, no add-ons needed.

🔐

Encrypted password vault

AES-256 with a key only you hold. Syncs as ciphertext, with cards and addresses too.

🔄

Sync that you own

Your own Docker server, or a private encrypted folder in your Google Drive or OneDrive.

🪟

Tabs, stacks & split view

Stack tabs, drag them side by side, search every open tab, and see tabs from your other devices.

📖

Reader view

Ads, sidebars and clutter out. Just the article, with text size, font and light/sepia/dark themes.

🎨

Themes & wallpapers

Dark, light and custom themes that update live. Change the look without restarting anything.

👥

Profiles

Separate profiles with their own data, avatars and lock screens. Work, home, kids, whatever you need.

🏠

Home network mode

Reach devices on your home LAN from anywhere through your own server. Router admin pages, NAS, cameras.

On your phone

Native Android, same you.

A real Android app, not a wrapper: your speed dials, bookmarks and tabs are right there.

Sync

One browser. Every device.

Your data goes to a server you control, never to ours, because there isn't one.

  • Bookmarks, history & tabs follow you; deletions sync too.
  • Passwords travel encrypted; the server only ever sees ciphertext.
  • Settings & themes carry over to new devices in seconds.
  • Prefer the cloud? Use a private folder in your own Google Drive or OneDrive instead.

Self-host

Your server in one minute.

Paste this into Dockhand, Dockge, Portainer or any Compose tool, open port 4242 and create your account.

services:
  mbrowser-sync:
    image: ghcr.io/flesheatingbug/mbrowser-sync:latest
    container_name: mbrowser-sync
    restart: unless-stopped
    environment:
      - PUID=1000
      - PGID=1000
      - TZ=Europe/London
      - TRUST_PROXY=true   # behind Nginx Proxy Manager / Traefik
    ports:
      - "4242:4242"
    volumes:
      - ./data:/config

Then in mbrowser: Settings → Sync → Server address. Full guide with reverse proxy and two-factor setup on GitHub ↗
