mbrowser

mbrowser is a privacy-focused web browser for Linux, Windows and Android. It keeps your bookmarks, history, open tabs, settings and an encrypted password vault in step across your devices, either through a sync server you host yourself or through a private folder in your own Google Drive or OneDrive.

Google Drive and OneDrive sync

When you choose Google Drive sync, mbrowser asks for one permission only: access to its own hidden application data folder in your Drive (drive.appdata). It cannot see, read or change any of your other files. Everything it stores there is encrypted on your device with a passphrase that only you know, before it is uploaded.

See the privacy policy for the details. The source code is at github.com/flesheatingbug/mbrowser.
