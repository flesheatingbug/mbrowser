What mbrowser is

mbrowser is a web browser that runs on your own devices. The developer does not operate any server that receives your data, and the app does not send your browsing data, bookmarks, passwords or any analytics to the developer or to any third party.

Google user data

If you turn on Google Drive sync, mbrowser requests a single permission: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata. This gives the app access only to a hidden folder in your Google Drive that belongs to the app. The app cannot see, list, read, change or delete any other file in your Drive.

  • What is stored there: your bookmarks, browsing history, list of open tabs, browser settings and your password vault, as files encrypted on your device (AES-256-GCM, with a key derived from a passphrase you choose) before upload. Google only holds encrypted files.
  • How it is used: only to synchronise those items between your own devices. Nothing is used for advertising, analytics, profiling or machine learning, and nothing is shared with or sold to anyone.
  • Where credentials are kept: the sign-in tokens and the encryption key stay on your device, protected by the operating system's secure storage where available. The developer never receives them and has no access to your data.

mbrowser's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Microsoft OneDrive

OneDrive sync works the same way, using only the app's private folder in your OneDrive (Files.ReadWrite.AppFolder).

Your controls

You can sign out in Settings > Sync at any time, which deletes the tokens and key from your device. You can revoke access in your Google Account (Security > Third-party access) or Microsoft account, and delete the app's hidden folder data from there. Uninstalling the app removes all local data.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page. Questions can be raised at github.com/flesheatingbug/mbrowser/issues.
